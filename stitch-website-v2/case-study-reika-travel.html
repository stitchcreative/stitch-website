Modular Travel Pack
3-in-1 Carry System

A travel pack that carries like a backpack, converts to a duffel, and handles like a side-carry bag. Straps tuck away completely. Built from DI-TEX 840D Carbonated Nylon, engineered for anti-scratch performance. Kickstarter funded, shipped to backers in 15+ countries.

Challenge

01

One bag that carries three ways, without compromising any of them

Convertible bags often fail at this: they try to do multiple things and end up doing none of them well. The travel pack mode has compromised structure because the duffel handles get in the way. The duffel mode is awkward because the backpack straps bulk out the back. Conversion is slow or fiddly, so people stop doing it.

The brief for this project was specific: design a bag that converts between backpack, duffel, and side-carry handles genuinely, where the straps tuck away completely in each mode so the bag looks and functions as if it was designed for that carry mode. Not a backpack with duffel handles attached. An actual three-mode carry system.

The additional constraint was Reika's materials standard: performance fabrics engineered to spec, not commodity nylon. DI-TEX, their proprietary 840D Carbonated Nylon, was the baseline. And the product had to work as the hero item in a Kickstarter campaign, which means the design story had to be legible to a backer who would only read a 90-second pitch.

Goals

02

What the Travel Pack system had to deliver

  • Clean backpack carry with tucked straps that don't affect duffel or side-carry modes
  • Functional duffel mode: top handles that take weight comfortably without the backpack straps getting in the way
  • Side-carry mode via integrated handles on the short axis of the bag
  • Accessories range: camera cube integration, shoulder strap, hip belt, rain cover, accessory straps
  • DI-TEX 840D Carbonated Nylon shell (anti-scratch engineered), 210D Nylon lining, VELEX anti-scratch lining in the laptop pocket, four-way stretch, performance mesh
  • Vietnam production, audited factory, AQL QC throughout

Design

03

The strap tuck system is what makes the conversion actually work

The design challenge on a multi-mode carry bag is almost always the same: how do you hide the straps for each mode that isn't currently active. If the straps aren't cleanly concealed, the bag reads as a backpack with add-ons rather than a genuine convertible system.

We designed a tuck-away system that stows the backpack straps into a sealed back panel pocket, making them completely flush and invisible when the bag is in duffel or side-carry mode. The conversion is fast enough to be practical rather than theatrical. The duffel handles and side carry handles are built into the structure of the bag body, not added as an afterthought.

The Camera Cube compatibility wasn't an afterthought. We designed the travel pack internal dimensions around the cube from the start.

The accessories range was designed as an ecosystem, not as a set of optional additions. The camera cube integrates via internal gatekeepers. The shoulder strap and hip belt use the same attachment points as the backpack harness. The rain cover stores in its own internal pocket. The system makes sense because every component was designed knowing what the others needed to do.

Engineering

04

Three carry modes, one hardware system

Technical specification
Shell fabric: 840D Carbonated Nylon (DI-TEX, anti-scratch engineered)
Interior: 210D Nylon lining, VELEX anti-scratch in laptop pocket, four-way stretch, performance mesh
Carry modes: Backpack / Duffel / Side-carry handle
Strap system: Full tuck-away back panel storage
Camera cube: Gatekeeper integration system
Hardware: YKK zips throughout

The gatekeeper system for Camera Cube integration uses internal attachment loops at specific positions inside the main compartment. The cube clips in, pulls tight, and doesn't move in transit. The same positions anchor the optional shoulder strap configuration when using the bag in duffel mode.

Materials

05

DI-TEX fabric system — engineered for performance, not just specification

The shell uses DI-TEX — 840D Carbonated Nylon engineered by Reika specifically for anti-scratch resistance. The denier weight and construction give the bag durability appropriate for frequent travel without adding unnecessary bulk. Interior lining is 210D Nylon throughout.

The laptop pocket uses VELEX as an anti-scratch lining — protecting devices from abrasion in daily use. Four-way stretch and performance mesh are used in the harness system where movement and ventilation matter. The accessories range (camera cube, shoulder strap, hip belt) uses the same DI-TEX shell, so the full system reads as one product family rather than a bag with add-ons bolted on.

YKK hardware throughout. Zip tape specification consistent across the main bag and all accessories to ensure the same hand-feel and durability at every access point.

Production

06

Vietnam production with accessories range coordinated in parallel

The main pack and accessories range were produced in Vietnam. Coordinating a multi-SKU accessories range alongside a hero product in production adds scheduling and QC complexity: the camera cube has to be tested inside the bag, the shoulder strap attachment has to be checked against the bag attachment points, and all components have to ship together.

We ran fit checks across the accessories range partway through the production run, pairing samples of each accessory with samples of the main bag to confirm integration tolerances held. When they did, production continued. When they didn't, we resolved it before committing to the full run.

All units inspected to AQL 2.5. Strap tuck-away system operation and zip function treated as critical attributes. Camera cube gatekeeper integration checked on a sample basis throughout the run.

A modular travel system that shipped to backers in over 15 countries

The Modular Travel Pack launched on Kickstarter, funded, and shipped to backers. The three carry modes work as designed: backpack straps tuck away cleanly, the duffel handles take weight without the harness getting in the way, the side-carry handles are positioned correctly for the weight distribution of a packed bag.

The accessories range expanded the product story: not just a travel bag, a complete carry ecosystem for photographers and travellers who want a system rather than a collection of separate products.
