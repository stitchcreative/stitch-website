How it works

From brief to production.
In 3–6 months.

Industry average: 18 months Stitch average: 3–6 months Why: because we're based in Asia
Step 01

Brief & Concept

Product definition, user research, commercial targets, concept generation. We align before anything goes to design.

Step 02

Design & Tech Pack

Sketch exploration, CAD, material and hardware selection. Full technical documentation that factories can actually build from.

Step 03

Sampling

Samples reviewed on-site by our team. Direct factory dialogue. Refinement rounds are faster when we're in the same room.

Step 04

Production

On-site factory oversight throughout the run. Issues resolved before they become shipment problems. Pre-shipment inspection available on request.

Step 05

Delivery

Goods shipped, documentation complete, logistics coordinated. Your product arrives exactly as sampled.
