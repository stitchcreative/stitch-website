How we work

No hand-offs.
No lost context.

Most agencies design the product and hand the spec pack to someone else. We own the chain — design, development, factory, QC — which is why things don't fall apart between phases.

01
Discovery

Brief, scope, and strategic alignment

Every project starts with a call — not an email form. We need to understand what you're building, why, and what success looks like. The brief determines everything downstream. Getting it wrong here is expensive.

Duration
1–2 weeks
Deliverable
Scope document + project proposal
Your input
Brief, references, cost targets
02
Concept Development

Design with manufacturing intent baked in

Sketch, material direction, hardware, construction logic — all in parallel. We design it knowing exactly how it will be made and what it costs at scale.

Duration
3–5 weeks
Deliverable
Concept sketches, material board, initial BOM
Milestones
Concept sign-off
03
Technical Development

Spec pack, patterns, and factory briefing

Approved concept becomes a full technical specification — flat patterns, construction details, dimensioned drawings, hardware specs, materials callouts. Everything a factory needs to produce a correct first sample without a phone call.

Duration
4–6 weeks
Deliverable
Full spec pack, tech pack, factory brief
Factory involvement
Costing, feedback loop begins
04
Sampling & Refinement

Proto rounds managed end to end

First samples come back. We inspect them against the spec, document every deviation with photo evidence, and build a consolidated revision pack for the factory. You see the feedback summary and approve the direction before we send anything back.

Duration
6–12 weeks (2–3 rounds)
Deliverable
Revision reports, golden sample
Milestone
Pre-production sign-off
05
Production

On-the-ground quality control throughout

Production runs with us managing the factory relationship directly. In-line inspections at 20% and 80% of the run. Final random inspection to AQL 2.5 before anything ships. Any defects resolved at source — not via email chains six weeks after delivery.

Duration
6–14 weeks (volume dependent)
Inspection standard
AQL 2.5 throughout
Deliverable
Goods on ship, QC report
06
Post-Launch

Iterate, scale, and repeat

Product in market, feedback coming in. We use the first production cycle to build a better one — tighter tolerances on anything that showed variability, adjusted specs based on real-world feedback, optimised costing for re-orders at higher volume.

Focus
Re-order optimisation, cost reduction
Benefit
Faster subsequent cycles
Outcome
Measurable COGS improvement

How we think
01

Manufacturing first

Every design decision is made with the factory in mind. If it can't be made consistently and cost-effectively at scale, it doesn't go in the spec.

02

On-site, not remote

Factory problems get solved at the factory. We don't manage production from a spreadsheet. We're there when it matters.

03

Honest timelines

We'll tell you upfront what's realistic. A compressed timeline that misses is worse than an honest one that hits. We don't sell you a date we can't hold.

04

Cost as a design constraint

Target margin isn't something you think about after design is done. It's a design constraint from day one. We build to the number.

05

One point of accountability

You have one contact. That person knows the design, the factory, the spec, and the QC history. Nothing falls through the gaps because there are no gaps.

06

Compounding returns

The second product in our system is always faster and cheaper than the first. Factory relationships, material leverage, and institutional knowledge compound over time.
