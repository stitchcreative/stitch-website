Track 3
Production management & QC
Scoped per project
Volume, factory location, and QC approach all shape the scope. We quote after a short conversation — accurate numbers only.
What this covers
Once you have an approved sample and production-ready tech pack, this is the service that gets it manufactured correctly. We manage the factory relationship, oversee production, and make sure what ships matches what was signed off.
Scope can include any combination of
  • Factory sourcing and qualification (if not already established)
  • PO placement and production scheduling
  • Raw material inspection
  • Inline QC at key production milestones
  • On-floor factory management during production runs
  • External QC partner sourcing and management
  • AQL 2.5 end-of-line inspection
  • Defect reporting, remediation management, and re-inspection
  • Shipping documentation and coordination

Volume, factory location, and QC approach all affect the cost significantly. A short conversation gets you an accurate number.

What's not included

Everything below sits outside our project fees and is charged separately or passed through at cost. We flag all of these during scoping so there are no surprises mid-project.

✈️
Domestic and international travel
Factory visits, sample reviews, and overseas inspections are charged at cost. We agree whether travel is necessary before booking anything.
🧵
Sampling and prototype costs
Factory sample charges are passed through at cost. Agreed and confirmed before each sample is placed — no surprises mid-project.
🏭
Production costs (FOB)
Factory production costs are invoiced directly to you. We never mark up production. Our fee covers management — that's always clearly separated in your quote.
🚢
Freight, duties and import costs
Shipping, customs clearance, and import duties sit with you. We can recommend freight partners and support documentation — but these costs are outside our scope.
Common questions

Things people usually ask first

If yours isn't here, ask it on a call.

Do I need a minimum order quantity to work with you?
No minimum. We work with brands placing their first 300-unit run and established ranges doing 10,000+ units. What matters is that the project is clearly scoped and the economics make sense. We'll tell you honestly if we don't think they do.
Can I do Track 1 now and add production management later?
Yes. The tracks are designed to be modular. Some brands start with a tech pack and factory intro, take those materials and manage production themselves, then come back to us for QC on the first run. Others do the full journey end to end. Either way works.
Why is production management bespoke rather than fixed price?
Because the variables are too significant to quote blind. A single product run at a nearby Vietnam factory is a different scope to managing three products across two factories in China with inline visits and external QC. Quoting a fixed price for that would either mean we're overcharging you or working for too little — neither is good. We'd rather have a quick conversation and give you an accurate number.
What if the number of sample rounds goes over what was agreed?
Each sample round has a defined purpose and test list. If a product is genuinely complex and requires additional rounds beyond what was scoped, we'll discuss it before proceeding. Any change to scope is agreed in writing before we commit to it.
Do you mark up factory or sampling costs?
No. Factory production and sample costs are passed through at the actual rate. You see the factory invoice. Our fee is separate and clearly itemised. That transparency is deliberate — it means you can always verify what you're paying for.
I already have a tech pack. Can I just come in for production management?
Yes. If you have production-ready tech packs and an approved pre-production sample, we can pick up from there. We'll review your existing documentation before quoting to make sure there are no gaps that would cause problems on the factory floor.
